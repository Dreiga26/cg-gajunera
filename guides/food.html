Eating Well on a Student Budget

Your first year is busy: new classes,new friends, and long days on campus. It is easy to skip meals or spend your whloe allowance on milk tea. Eating regular,simple meals helps you focus in class,and you do not need a big budget to do it.

Where to Eat Near Campus

You have more option than you think.Here are the usual go-to spots:

  • The school canteen for quick rice meals between classes
  • Carinderias outside the gate for cheap, filling ulam and rice
  • Your own baon from home, which is the cheapest choice of all
  • Convenience stores for emergency snacks, not for every meal

How to plan your weekly food budget

  1. Check your allowance you have for the whole week
  2. Set aside money for fare and school needs first
  3. Divide what is left by the number of school days
  4. pack baon on at least two days to stretch your budget.
  5. Keep a small amount for emergency,like a surprise project

    6. Quick Tips

    Always bring a refillable water bottle.Water Stations on campus are free, and you will save a lot compared to buying bottled water every day.
